## Short description

> Sort Indonesian street food on warung shelves: match three alike, save fresh food before it spoils and fill Grandma's book across 50 island levels.

(149 characters.)

## Long description

> Welcome to the warung! The shelves of an Indonesian street stall are a jumble of satay, iced tea, rambutan, ketupat and dozens of other treats and treasures. Drag them from shelf to shelf and put three of a kind together to clear them. Behind each row more items wait their turn, so plan your moves and keep space free.
>
> **Keep it fresh.** Some foods are fresh: a little price tag counts down their moves. Make their triple in time for bonus points, or watch them spoil.
>
> **Market days.** The game follows the real Javanese five-day market week, the pasaran. Each day (Legi, Pahing, Pon, Wage, Kliwon) favours a different food, and its triples pay extra coins.
>
> **Grandma's Book.** Every item you match fills a page of Buku Nenek: its picture, its name in English and Indonesian, where it comes from and a short, true fact. Complete a page for a gift.
>
> Travel across the archipelago through 50 hand-made levels and ten themed islands: Javanese culture, Independence Day, the night market, the seas of Nusantara, sports, traditional games, Maluku spices, rice fields, Indonesian wildlife and a final festival. New ideas arrive as you go: belts that carry stacks past the shelves, locked shelves, shelves that close once emptied and a crate that wanders round the board.
>
> Chain triples for combos, earn up to three stars per level, open star chests and use boosters to freeze the clock, remove a triple or shuffle the board. Every level can be beaten without them. Your progress is saved in your browser.
>
> Easy to pick up, satisfying to master. Selamat bermain!

## Controls

**Mouse**
- Drag an item onto another shelf, or click an item and then click a free slot.
- Click an item on a moving belt to take it, then click where it goes (or drag it there).
- P: pause (or the pause button).

**Touch**
- Drag an item onto another shelf, or tap an item and then tap a free slot.
- Tap an item on a moving belt to take it, then tap where it goes (or drag it there).
